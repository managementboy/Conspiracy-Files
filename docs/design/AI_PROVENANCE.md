# AI Content and Provenance Policy

## Development-time AI
AI may assist with draft letters, diary entries, descriptions and template variants. Every canonical shipped asset requires human approval. Approved assets are treated as authored game content in-fiction.

The project should disclose in repository/Workshop documentation that AI-assisted text may be used, without adding immersion-breaking labels to every item. Future content-pack manifests should include provenance metadata once packs exist.

Approved Workshop wording:

> Some narrative text in Conspiracy-Files was drafted with AI assistance and then reviewed, edited, and approved by the project owner. Runtime AI is not required and is not used to create authoritative story facts.

## Runtime AI
Optional only. Runtime outputs are interpretations/summaries, never evidence. The data model records provenance. Normal notebook styling may remain unified, but an optional provenance toggle can show whether text/linkage came from game/authored fact, player note, system inference or runtime AI.

No provider credentials are stored in saves or committed to the repository.
