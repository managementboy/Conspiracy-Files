## What a thread would be here (proposal, not built)

Findings that share a place, or are joined by a connection link. The survivor's
word stays "thread"; wording stays first person with doubt ("what I think I am
following"). Open = the survivor is still following it; closed = the survivor put
it down, their choice, never "solved". Cost: one small root, id plus flag per
thread, registered in `SaveBudget.tags`.
