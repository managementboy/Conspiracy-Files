# Is the 500 kB a real limit? No — and we are nowhere near the real one

Written because the owner asked whether the save format cannot hold the full map
catalogue, and whether that is a technical fact or a decision. **It is a decision,
and the evidence behind it says our shortfall is trivial.**

## It is not the save format

The game's own save format round-tripped **44 megabytes** intact — 100,000
records, every one verified, checksum exact (`docs/research/T1_MODDATA_PERSISTENCE.md`).
The format is not the constraint and never was.

## What the 500 kB actually is

A **self-imposed performance ceiling** (`P4-R17`), set because at 44 MB saving
froze the game for about nine seconds. The stated reason is exactly that:
*"technically serialisable larger states caused unacceptable synchronous
stalls."* Not a format limit — a decision about how long a player should wait.

## The numbers it was set from

| records | file bytes | save | validation | responsiveness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | 442,558 | 449 ms | 363 ms | no stalls |
| 10,000 | **4,432,276** | **512 ms** | 1,012 ms | **no stalls** |
| 100,000 | 44,419,437 | 9,177 ms | 9,057 ms | stalled |

**Read the middle row.** Four and a half megabytes saved in half a second with no
measured stall at all. That is **nine times** our entire budget, comfortable.

The ceiling was then set at 500 kB — just above the *smallest* sample, not
anywhere near where the trouble starts. The gap between "comfortable" and
"painful" is a hundredfold, and **nothing in between was ever measured.**

## What that means for the map catalogue

The full 125-destination catalogue needs about **555 kB** — 54 kB over a 500 kB
ceiling, when 4.4 MB has been measured as comfortable.

So the honest position, which corrects what I wrote in the plan and in
`WHAT_WE_ARE_BUILDING`: **coverage is not blocked by the save at all.** It is
blocked by a conservative number we chose, roughly nine times below the nearest
measured problem.

## What I would not do

Abolish the budget. Three reasons it still earns its keep:

1. **The test used flat synthetic records**, not our nested case structures. Ours
   validate on every write, and validation was the *slower* half of that row —
   a second of it at 4.4 MB.
2. **A budget is what stopped this project shipping a broken save before.** A
   ten-case save once measured 521 kB against this ceiling and the ceiling caught
   it (`WHAT_DO_I_MAKE_OF_IT.md`).
3. **Save time is not the only cost** — load time and memory ride along with it,
   and neither was the thing measured here.

## What I would do instead

**Raise the ceiling to a measured figure, rather than ration maps.**

One measurement answers it: take our *real* save — actual cases, actual ledger,
actual paperwork — at roughly 600 kB, 800 kB and 1 MB, and record the save and
validation time at each. If it looks like the middle row above, the coverage
problem does not exist and the whole rationing question disappears. If our
structures are dearer than flat records, we learn the real curve and set the
ceiling honestly on our own data instead of on a synthetic sample from months
ago.

That is a single night's work and it is cheaper than one town of authored
paperwork.

## What changes in the plan

- §14.1's options were: shrink the case archive, ration, or spend the reserve.
  **A fourth option joins them and is better than all three: measure the ceiling
  and raise it on evidence.**
- "The full catalogue does not fit" stays true **of the current ceiling** and must
  not be repeated as a fact about the game.
- The gated pilot is unaffected. It fits either way, at 4,846 bytes of 7,826.
