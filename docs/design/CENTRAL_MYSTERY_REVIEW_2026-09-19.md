## Confirmed premise

The search for an explanation of the survivor's isolation is the heart of the mod: why am I alone in Knox, am I the only survivor, who is responsible, why is nobody coming, and what was the helicopter doing? The owner reaffirmed that there is no definitive explanation of the Knox Event's cause. **The question remains central even though the mod never supplies a final answer.** Do not implement a secret canonical culprit, cause, final solution or hidden correct theory. The survivor's apparent isolation is a question, not proof that no other survivors exist.

Local mysteries should build understanding relevant to this search. They can establish particular actions, people, motives and consequences without establishing responsibility for the entire catastrophe. Distinguish observed facts, document claims and player interpretations. A connection must have actual evidential substance; repeating an organisation name or sprinkling ominous phrases is insufficient.

## Confirmed discovery routes

1. **Personal opening:** our own deliberately placed, plausible evidence ties the survivor to the starting place and raises why they arrived there. Starting skills influence selection/observations. No dependency on randomly finding a matching vanilla flyer, map or distant occupation-specific business. Biography boundaries remain open.
2. **Naturally discovered annotated maps:** every discovered annotated map generates a mystery. Its message/marks give a reason to travel; evidence at the destination provides a meaningful clear or ambiguous local ending. Intermediate stops are optional, not compulsory padding. This is a coverage requirement, not a claim that all 125 designs are currently implementable.
3. **Flyers and brochures:** naturally found media can provide additional opportunities. Whether every flyer must generate a mystery, and the brochure policy, remain open. Occupation-specific media are not a prerequisite.

The exact meaning of discovered (seen/read/recorded), duplicate-copy behavior and scheduling remain open. Reading as the eligibility trigger was an assistant recommendation, not owner approval. Every-map coverage must coexist with Q11's manageable attention: pending investigations are an implementation proposal, not silently dropped maps or forced simultaneous active cases.

## Proposed case authoring contract

For each mystery specify: initiating question; source map/print or mod clue; verified destination and carriers; local facts the evidence supports; what remains ambiguous; the central question affected; the concrete new connection; and what the player still cannot conclude. Local resolution and central contribution may use the same evidence. No fixed number of clues is implied.

An ambiguous ending must supply enough evidence for plausible interpretations; missing content is not ambiguity. A broader connection is part of the mystery's purpose, not a substitute for the local payoff. Do not manufacture an endless mandatory cliffhanger, universal villain or final theory score. Cross-case connections must come from observed/recovered evidence, not undiscovered author metadata.

## Two working examples — proposals, not approved fiction

**LouisvilleStashMap15, Natalie/art gallery:** vanilla asks the finder to protect the gallery's treasures. Proposed packing list, caretaker note and inventory establish that selected works were protected but transport did not collect them. An additional reassignment record could show evacuation transport priorities changed before public cancellation. Local payoff: what became of the preservation effort. Central contribution: evidence about who knew and whose evacuation was prioritised. It does not establish the Knox cause, Natalie's final fate beyond source claims, or that everybody was abandoned by the same actor. Validate real gallery carriers; do not claim source rectangles prove access or spawn bulk artworks into already checked rooms.

**WorldStashMap11, faulty dish:** vanilla mentions Dish 3/Tower 2, a signal fault around 6 GHz, suspected power trouble and a Lexington test. Proposed maintenance/test/supervisor records establish an unresolved fault and deliberate closure of testing. A separately proposed acknowledged distress message could make continued listening plausible. Local payoff: what happened to the investigation. Central contribution: who could communicate and why assistance did not follow. Concealed failure and deliberate interference can remain competing explanations. A received acknowledgement is not proof of rescue capacity or knowledge of all Knox. No claim that this is Relay Site 31 or the separately supplied Brandenburg station. Exact communications fiction needs premise review.

These examples supersede the earlier framing of the larger connection as merely a bonus. They do not authorise writing these precise documents into the game yet.
