# Occupation mysteries and vanilla-media integration — Linux planning package

**Status: design proposals and verification plan, 19 September 2026. No implementation or new live-engine verification.** Owner explicitly requested planning on Windows and separate development on the Linux laptop. Premises below need agreement under Q27 before new story content is developed. The existing [media inventory](../research/vanilla-print-2026-09-19/README.md) is reused, not rebuilt.

## 1. Product boundary

Version 1.0 enriches ordinary single-player survival on the vanilla Build 42 map. Offline authored stories and local rules remain the approach. The next development focus is the personal opening, then survival connections, then broader loop improvements. Technical checks establish milestone acceptance; the owner decides when the feature set is ready for long-term play.

Every opening asks a modest personal question: **why was I heading here, carrying this, or expected at this place?** Do not invent the survivor's family, moral character, military unit, criminal acts or prior employer by default. Employment adverts can suggest an application or appointment rather than assert an employment history. A concrete object or record must establish any personal connection. The unanswered extent of the survivor's authored biography remains a premise-approval boundary.

Use actual starting skills to rank eligible themes; occupation is a useful prior, not a separate fixed campaign. Traits can change starting capabilities. Snapshot the character's starting profile once; later training changes available interactions, not the past. Never make every occupation travel to one distant named business immediately. Start with a local clue and reachable local progression; named distant destinations become later travel when appropriate. A vanilla document supplies a destination or testimony, not automatic proof of our invented connection.

## 3. Proposed gameplay loop

1. **Local personal trace:** discover one small plausible item explaining a recent appointment, collection or detour. Initial recognition follows the existing Search/Look it over/Inspect flow. The system must not require a specific distant flyer to randomly spawn before the player can progress.
2. **Concrete lead:** evidence identifies a person, reference or place. A relevant vanilla flyer/map can identify that place or corroborate its address. Record the lead with its source; no generic suspicion bookmark is required.
3. **Destination opportunity:** prepare one appropriate follow-up at the specified place after technical eligibility checks. A read document never guarantees a living contact or validates its claims. The player travels and investigates normally.
4. **Meaningful result:** an observed match, contradiction or physical finding answers the bounded question. A conclusion cites the evidence that supports it. Other connections may remain open in the continuing web.
5. **Later interaction:** when a case is designed to require skill/tool X at object Y, explain the obstacle through an observation. Meeting the requirements reveals evidence or changes a specifically designed object. No universal bypass is implied; exact skill levels, consumables and recipes are future tuning decisions.

### Interaction contract

Every designed interaction lists: identifiable target; observation explaining its relevance; prerequisites; reachable approach square; timed action and cancellation conditions; tool wear/consumption; one stable result ID; repeat behavior; save/reload recovery. Cancellation before completion creates no evidence. Completion commits its result once. Reload after completion must not duplicate the item or charge a tool twice. Preserve the normal use of the object where possible. Do not invent a new item type or crafting recipe merely because a premise mentions a tool.

### Plausibility rule from the dust masks

A detailed search can reveal a key, folded note, small component or the meaning of an existing object. A large batch cannot appear in a place already plainly inspected. Prefer initially placed bulk objects or recognition of an existing object over late bulk insertion. If prior visibility cannot be established, use another eligible unobserved small carrier or keep placement pending. Previously read location references remain fixed even if an undiscovered connecting clue is re-offered elsewhere. Loot-window inspection counts as a possible knowledge boundary, not just our own Investigate Area action.

## 5. Seen, read and recorded: separate facts

These are independent monotonic observations per character/source, not one global three-value flag. A combined Inspect may commit read and recorded together, but their meanings remain separate.

| State | Proposed observable threshold | What it permits | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seen | A relevant item/visual is actually presented through a verified player-visible surface. A hidden container scan is insufficient. | Remember physical exposure; prevent implausible relocation of something the player could already have seen. | Reading its prose, believing it, or logging evidence. |
| Read / presented | The real reading/inspection action reaches its verified successful presentation point. This operationally means content was shown, not that a human understood every word. | Preserve communicated destinations/facts as known claims; offer an actionable lead when supported. | Truth of the claim, completed travel, or automatic organiser entry. |
| Recorded | Existing successful Inspect/record action stores the source and relevant facts in our investigation record. | Link evidence, resume the lead, derive supported conclusions. | Automatic inheritance by another survivor. |

Record timestamps, source design/copy and observation route when obtainable. Closing immediately, cancelled actions, darkness, sleep, queue interruption and failed UI opening need explicit outcomes from tests. Merely possessing an item is not reading it. Reopening already read material is idempotent. Reading a second copy can record separate provenance without duplicating the same conclusion.

Seen/read claims constrain world consistency even if never recorded. Treat a known destination as learned once its content is successfully presented. A debug scan never produces seen/read states. If reliable exposure detection is unavailable, conservatively freeze a possibly exposed placement rather than assume it was unnoticed.

For Q17, a new survivor starts with separate knowledge. Recovering predecessor evidence or organiser grants access according to the eventual recovery rules; acquiring a generic organiser or opening an unrelated map marker must not import another character's investigation. Current-build reload preserves all three observations. Q29 removes legacy migration obligations, not current-save integrity.

## 6. Destination and carrier contract

The destination rectangle is a search boundary, not a placement location. Resolve the semantic destination first: the gallery Target mark at 12546,1393 differs from its stash building anchor. Near-zero placeholder anchors are forbidden as destinations. Multi-rectangle brochures may describe a whole complex; assign a specific relevant sub-location only after verifying it.

For every proposed carrier establish:

- correct destination, building/room/floor and current loaded state;
- a traversable approach from a real player-reachable entry, including stairs, doors and possible obstacles;
- actual interaction reach to the container/object, not only walkability of its square;
- carrier identity, capacity/type suitability, ownership/state and whether it was previously observed;
- vanilla stash preparation state and possible imminent inventory changes;
- a single committed evidence identity and a fresh reach/access check at interaction time.

The current `ReachabilityAdapter` accepts only completed bounded search results. Reuse that boundary; unfinished, oversized or unloaded searches remain **unproven**. Cached reachability is not permanent proof after doors, walls, stairs, vehicles or containers change. Separate **reachable now**, **intentionally gated by a designed tool/key**, **unloaded/pending**, and **invalid**. A gated destination requires a designed means of access; it cannot silently pass the reachable check.

First candidates: Circuital Healing's real service storage; a local ordinary office or household carrier for the opening; the relay site's actual service structure; gallery office/storage. Bunker levels follow later. No exact shelf or basement is approved by a brochure drawing. Failed destination resolution keeps the lead pending or reports a factual obstacle; it never silently changes a destination the player knows.

## 7. Vanilla stash timing: measure before choosing the insertion point

Source definitions include `spawnTable`, `spawnOnlyOnZed`, `daysToSpawn`, barricades, zombies, containers and occasional traps. This shows configured effects, not the engine's execution order. Do not assume map creation, reading, reveal-on-map, first building load and first container opening are equivalent triggers.

Proposed lifecycle for our planner: candidate destination → wait for relevant vanilla preparation to settle → resolve carrier and reachability → reserve evidence ID → commit once → verify persistence. If there is no reliable preparation signal, use an independently safe carrier or postpone; do not add an arbitrary timer and call it safe.

Linux comparison must use clean disposable saves/copies with the same build and setup. Observe (a) no map obtained; (b) map obtained but unread; (c) map reading completed; (d) reveal used; (e) destination loaded; (f) container opened. Repeat with destination visited before obtaining the map and with save/reload between milestones. Capture item IDs/counts and relevant stash state before/after each step. Include a normal cache, information-only map, `spawnOnlyOnZed`, day-window case and trapped case. Never invoke stash preparation a second time just to discover whether it happened.

The new clue must survive vanilla preparation without duplication, replacement of vanilla loot, or repeated zombies/barricades/traps. If tests disprove this at one insertion point, change that integration point and rerun only the affected cases.
