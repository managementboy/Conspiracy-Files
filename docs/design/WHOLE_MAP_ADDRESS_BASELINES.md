# Whole-map house numbers: the starting street of every town (for the owner to check)

Status 2026-10-02. The numbers themselves are built and shipped in both mods
(`Generated/AddressBook.lua`, 370 KB, 6,796 houses, 37 areas); see
`WHOLE_MAP_ADDRESSES.md` section 9. This page is the list you asked to see: for
each town, which street the numbering counts out from, and how the game chose it.

## How to read it

- Numbers count outward from the town's **starting street**. In a town with a
  Main Street, that is Main Street ("Main St rule"; towns with a north/south or
  east/west split use both halves). Otherwise the **longest street** in the town
  is used. Highways and railways are never a starting street.
- "No street within 60" are houses too far from any named street; they get no
  number (as today). "Considered" counts homes and businesses; basements,
  garage- or shed-only buildings and buildings with no rooms are left out.
- Areas called "unnamed area N near X" are the countryside between towns; they
  have no town name, so their addresses never add one.

## What to look at

1. Do the towns that use "longest street" (Ekron, Jefferson, LAA, MarchRidge,
   ValleyStation) have a starting street that feels like their centre? If one
   is wrong, tell me which street you would use; it is a one-line override.
2. Louisville has 113 houses, Muldraugh 77, Riverside 36 and Rosewood 42 with
   no named street near them: is that acceptable (they stay without a number)?
3. LAA only numbers 7 of 25 buildings (most of the airport lies outside the box).
