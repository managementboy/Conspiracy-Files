A TRAP WORTH RECORDING, because I nearly fell into it
  There are two sources of truth for a destination and they DISAGREE on this
  design. MapMediaCatalogue's raw binding gives MulStashMap16 a target of
  10628,9698; MapMediaDestinations' reviewed geometry overrides it to
  10612,9649. Reading either file alone gives the wrong answer - the catalogue
  says they do not share, the reviewed file says they do.

  MapMediaCatalogue.lua:395 settles it: `Destinations.apply` is run over every
  binding at load, so the reviewed geometry wins and they share. Any future
  measurement must be taken AFTER apply, from the catalogue, not from either
  file's literals.
