Save costs at 600 kB, 800 kB and 1 MB — they cannot be reached
measured 2026-09-21, source b8e89af / DEV-0.46.0-writing-rebuild

THE HANDOFF ASKS FOR
  "Measure actual save costs with real data around 600 kB, 800 kB and 1 MB."

THE ANSWER
  A save cannot get there. The mod's own case cap stops it at roughly a third
  of the budget, so those three measurements describe states the game will
  never be in.

HOW IT WAS MEASURED
  200 real generated cases (synthetic location fixture, seeds 1-200), stacked
  into one store and measured two ways: the mod's own estimator
  (Validator.estimateEncodedBytes) and an independent character count of what
  a plain-table store would serialise to. The second is deliberately NOT the
  estimator's arithmetic, or it would only agree with itself.

    cases      estimated    serialised   ratio
        5         116511         30065    3.88
       10         232203         59930    3.87
       20         462447        119367    3.87
       40         925041        238776    3.87
       80        1860775        480260    3.87
      120        2807252        724525    3.87
      160        3766681        972118    3.87
      200        4714821       1216809    3.87

  Flat at 3.87x across a forty-fold range. That matches the one real in-game
  calibration taken earlier today - 102,418 estimated against 29,478 actual
  bytes, 3.47x - so the estimator overstates by roughly this much everywhere,
  not just at small sizes.

  Per case: about 23,124 estimated bytes, about 5,975 actual.

THE CEILING
  SuccessiveCases.MAX_CASES = 16, MAX_ACTIVE = 4.

    16 cases  ~369,984 estimated bytes  = 37% of MAX_ENCODED_BYTES (1,000,000)
              ~95,603 ACTUAL bytes

  So the largest save the mod can produce is about a third of the estimated
  budget and about a tenth of it in real bytes. 600 kB estimated would need
  ~26 cases, 800 kB ~35, and 1 MB ~43 - all past a cap of 16.

  The campaign runs support this from the other end: real saves measured
  62,696 to 78,056 bytes with two cases and a full ledger, and unchanged to
  the byte across every reload.

WHAT THIS MEANS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
  The 1,000,000 figure is provisional and not an engine limit (the handoff
  says so), and nothing here argues for changing it. What it does say is that
  the budget is not currently a constraint on anything: the case cap binds
  first, by a factor of about three on the estimate and ten on real bytes.
  If the cap ever rises, this is the number to re-measure. Until then, a
  600 kB save is not a state to test for - it is a state to explain.

STATUS: requirement answered; the three measurements are not reachable.
