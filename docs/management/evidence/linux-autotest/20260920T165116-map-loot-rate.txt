QUESTION: the whole map-media feature begins when the player reads an annotated
map. How often does one actually turn up in ordinary play?

WHAT THE GAME CONTAINS (read from the installed files, not inferred):
  125 stash definitions, matching the mod's catalogue exactly, one for one.
  They ride on 13 ordinary map items (RosewoodMap, WestpointMap, MuldraughMap,
  RiversideMap, MarchRidgeMap, LouisvilleMap1-9). 47 of the 125 ride on
  RosewoodMap alone.
  Every one of those 13 appears in the loot tables - bins, closets, bags and
  procedural distributions - with weights from 0.005 to 20.
  The item scripts declare only which region a map shows. Nothing in Lua or in
  the item definition attaches a stash: that happens in Java at spawn, so it
  cannot be read, only measured.

WHAT WAS MEASURED (Muldraugh, 12 sample points, one fresh world):
  982 containers holding game-generated loot
    2 map items of any kind
    0 carrying a stash identity

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES:
  Map items are very rare in ordinary loot - roughly 2 per 1,000 containers in
  this sample. Since a stash map must first BE a map, that is a ceiling on how
  often the feature can begin by itself, whatever the stash attachment rate is.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH, and the sample is far too small to:
  - That loot-spawned maps never carry a stash. Two maps is not a sample; both
    being plain is unremarkable. The attachment rate remains unmeasured.
  - Anything about Louisville, West Point, Riverside, March Ridge or Rosewood:
    only Muldraugh was walked.
  - Anything about survivor houses, safehouses or the special containers that
    carry the heavier weights (one distribution entry weights RosewoodMap at 20).
    A targeted sample of those could look very different from a broad sweep.
