Map-media shared destinations: MEASUREMENT + mechanism test (no pass/fail gate)
source: b7357d2 (branch codex/map-media, DEV-0.45.1-map-media)

QUESTION: the plan calls "two designs, one destination" the step that proves the
product, because a contradiction needs two sources pointing at the same place.
Can that happen?

MEASURED IN A REAL GAME, over all 125 designs after indexing:
  123 destination buildings are hit.
  0 of them are hit by more than one design.

So no two shipped designs share a destination. The content for the
product-proving step does not exist. This is not a defect - it was never
authored - but it does mean the step cannot be run against what ships today.

Note the catalogue's own "relatedDestination" field is authored metadata, not a
computed relationship: exactly one design (EkronStashMap6) carries it, and it
does not correspond to any actual shared building. Do not read that field as
evidence that two designs meet somewhere.

MECHANISM, settled by test rather than left as an assumption:
  test/map_media_runtime.lua now widens the mock building so two designs' targets
  fall inside it, and asserts what happens. Both trails start, ONE offer of a
  single carrier serves both, two payoffs sit side by side, each keeping its own
  design identity, and a repeated offer adds nothing. Shown to fail: stopping the
  offer after the first design leaves one payoff and the test goes red.

  So the machinery is ready for the content. A building keyed to several designs
  places each one's payoff independently, and they do not displace each other.

WHAT REMAINS FOR A HUMAN:
  Choosing WHICH two places meet, and what the two records disagree about, is
  authoring, not engineering. Until that exists, contradiction between two
  sources at one destination has never happened in a running game.
