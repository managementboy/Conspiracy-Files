A BLANK SCREEN THAT IS NOT A DEFECT
  A screenshot taken ~30 minutes after opening showed the glass entirely blank.
  The organiser switches itself off after AUTO_OFF_MS = 180000 (three minutes),
  as a real Palm does. Reopening rendered the launcher correctly again
  (dev/eval/linux/runs/gate6-organiser-reopened.png). Reported here because the
  blank screen looks alarming and will be rediscovered otherwise.
