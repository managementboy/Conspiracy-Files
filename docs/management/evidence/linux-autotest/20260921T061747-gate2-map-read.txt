MADE THE GAME'S OWN WAY, NOT STAMPED BY THE TEST
  stash = StashSystem.getStash("BBurgStashMap1")
  map   = instanceItem(stash:getItem())
  StashSystem.doStashItem(stash, map)
  -> map:getStashMap() = "BBurgStashMap1"
  This is the same engine call the game's own stash debug panel uses.

OPENED THE WAY A PLAYER OPENS IT
  ISInventoryPaneContextMenu.createMenu(0,true,{map},10,10) built the real
  right-click menu; its "Read Map" option was selected exactly as a click does.
  -> returned without error

THE MOD RESPONDED: CONFIRMED
  coverage("BBurgStashMap1").active  false -> true, buildings = 1

AN ORDINARY MAP MUST NOT START A TRAIL: CONFIRMED
  A plain Base.RosewoodMap with no stash (getStashMap() = nil), read through
  the same menu path, left the active-trail count unchanged at 1. The mod
  discriminates correctly and ignores the ordinary maps a player will collect.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
  - The map was constructed in code, not found in world loot. Whether annotated
    maps appear often enough in ordinary play to carry the feature is a separate
    question about the game's distribution tables, measured earlier as roughly
    two map items per 1,000 containers.
  - No human right-clicked; the menu was built and its option invoked through
    the game's own functions.
  - Cancelled/repeated reads, duplicate physical copies, the three paced local
    parts, the destination payoff, a missed fragment on a later journey and
    destination-first discovery are NOT covered here.
