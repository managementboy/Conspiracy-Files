## Assignment

AddressRoads.lua derives 61 named road blocks by splitting installed street polylines at intersections with other streets. Railway-named Line records are excluded. Road geometry, not a junction display or verified road accessibility survey, defines trial intersections. The immutable revision is `muldraugh-address-1`.

Main Street and First Street supply the conceptual baseline corridors. The trial uses fixed internal axes x=10600 and y=9750. Block ranges on each street are ordered by distance from the appropriate corridor, with stable geometric tie breaks; addresses are fictional and do not reproduce official Louisville parcels. Curves, grade separations, duplicated road geometry and frontage remain limitations of this first geometry-based assignment.

All eligible buildings in the coverage rectangle are enumerated independently of the conspiracy shortlist. Basement definitions and standalone garage/shed-only buildings are excluded. Nearest straight-ish street sections within 50 tiles of building center provide an association; equal-distance competing blocks/sides are rejected. This is a geometric assignment convention, not proof of front-door access. Buildings are ordered outward within each block side; odd north/east, even south/west. Each side has at most 49 slots; overflow groups are rejected rather than producing duplicate numbers. Detached outbuilding relationships are not guessed.

The complete address book is validated and committed once to `ConspiracyFiles.AddressBook.Muldraugh`; subsequent loads reuse it without reseeding or renumbering. Address-book budget 400 KB; combined with the generated case, commit must stay below 500 KB. Generation holds no permanent Java object references. New maps, changed indexing revisions or incompatible builds refuse saved-book reuse; migration and added-building support are outside this trial.

## Map overlay

Installed ISMap:initMapData calls MapUtils.revealKnownArea / WorldMapVisited:setKnownInSquares. Inspection of the installed JAR confirms public isKnown(int,int) and isKnown(int,int,int,int); isKnown delegates to hasFlags with the known flag and a one-unit padded query. Actual Lua callability and visual masking remain live gates.

A cooperative ISWorldMap.render wrapper calls the original renderer first, then adds a temporary overlay. It does not mutate player annotations or set map-knowledge flags. Each label requires native known checks at building center and four corners. Labels refresh against knowledge every draw, including map forgetting; this follows native mask granularity rather than claiming exact per-house exploration. Close zoom (18+) and collision suppression keep labels readable. Up to 80 labels and a 1 ms draw deadline; actual frame cost needs measurement. Paper-map reveal enables labels in the revealed area, not outside it. Mini-map labels are not part of this trial.
