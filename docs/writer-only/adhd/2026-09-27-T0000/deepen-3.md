# Deepened idea 3 — shared records, different inference

Both accounts can use emergency orders, medical records, supply movement, and institutional omissions. Those evidence families do not belong to one theory by default. The distinction comes from what the fact changes: response timing and implementation for containment; program activity and the route to exposure for agriculture.

Map and flyer stories must remain compatible with either side being selected for a world. Their rare counter-side marks should be explainable as honest mismatch. Scene stories stay tied to the verified anchor; the unique scene receives two alternate readings at the same permitted anchor. No scene or spot becomes a lookup code.
