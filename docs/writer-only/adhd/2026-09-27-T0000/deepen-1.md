# Deepened idea 1 — response-account boundary

The response account needs a legible sequence without claiming an origin: officials receive incomplete or conflicting warning; formal controls and evacuation decisions change; the measures fail on the ground; later records or public explanations narrow responsibility. These are separate facts, so one missing form cannot stand in for the whole theory.

This makes the account expandable across institutions and ordinary situations. A clue may bear on a warning, an order, an implementation gap, or the aftermath. It need not prove all four. The account remains about response and its accounting, not a deliberate source event.
