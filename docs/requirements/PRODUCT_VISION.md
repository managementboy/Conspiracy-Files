# Product vision — reviewed 19 September 2026

## Current narrative interpretation — later review, 19 September 2026

Searching for an explanation of isolation in Knox is the central premise; no definitive cause is supplied. Local mysteries provide earned conclusions and substantive evidence relevant to that search. Our own clues establish the personal opening. Naturally discovered annotated maps each motivate travel and provide a destination payoff; universal flyer/brochure coverage remains open. See the [complete reassessment](../design/CENTRAL_MYSTERY_REVIEW_2026-09-19.md). Earlier optional-map or occupation-flyer opening proposals are superseded; underlying source research remains valid.


Version 1.0 enriches ordinary single-player survival on the vanilla Project Zomboid Build 42 map with offline investigations. The player finds physical evidence, records it in an organiser, recognises connections and gains reasons to explore. A continuing web of people and organisations supports local mysteries with meaningful conclusions, without explaining the cause of the Knox Event.

The next priority is a personal opening connected to the survivor's starting skills, followed by survival connections and improvements to the existing discovery loop. Early progression follows local exploration; later stories require travel and may require particular tools or skills. Writing uses fatalistic bureaucratic dark comedy.

A full survivor campaign, broader compatibility and PC/CD-ROM integration remain future possibilities. Technical checks passing establish milestone acceptance; the owner decides when the feature set is playable long term and reviews direction at each playable milestone. Breaking changes remain allowed with clear fresh-save notices.
