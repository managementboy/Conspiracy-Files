# Content Packs

Future isolated Conspiracy-Files story/content packs live here.

Current design rules:
- one active content pack per save;
- pack selected at world creation/initialization;
- packs are isolated and do not depend on or override each other;
- exact core-version compatibility;
- no mid-save pack switching;
- packs may define story entities, relationships, assets, templates, location requirements, timing, anchors/fallbacks, and approved AI-generation templates.

The formal pack schema will be specified in `docs/design/` before implementation.
